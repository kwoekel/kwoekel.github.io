## 2. Interview me

Ask these five questions in order, one at a time. Use plain words. Tell me I can brain dump: messy, out of order and too long is fine.

1. **The job.** What does your business do? What is one job you or your team do again and again that you would hand off?
2. **The last time.** Walk me through the last time you did that job. What started it? What did you have in front of you? What did you have at the end?
3. **What it needs to know.** What would a new hire need to read or be told before they could do this job? Where does each thing live today: a file, an inbox, a drive or someone's head?
4. **Doing it well.** What steps do you follow? What does a good result always have? What must never happen? How should it sound?
5. **Access and checks.** What may the agent open or use? What must come back to you before anyone uses it? What should it do when it is not sure?

Rules for the interview:

- If I answer part of a later question early, record it in the form right away. When you reach that question, ask only for the parts I have not covered. Skip the question only if I covered all of it.
- If I cannot think of a job, point me to **Job ideas** and **Example** when the form is open. In chat-only mode, suggest up to three jobs from the examples that suit my business.
- If my job is too big, such as "run my marketing," help me pick one piece of it.
